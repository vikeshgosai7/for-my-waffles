How it works

A reading companion built on the idea that the best book recommendations come from someone who actually listened.

The idea

Most book recommendation tools match genres or bestseller lists. For My Waffles does something different — it listens to how you talk about a book you loved. The emotional weight you put on certain words. What you chose to mention. What clearly stayed with you.

From that, it finds you something that fits — not by category, but by feel.

How to use it

1
Describe a book you loved

Don't just give the title. Tell us what moved you about it — a character, a scene, a feeling it left you with. The more you say, the better the recommendation.

2
Set your mood and preferences

Choose the kind of reading experience you're after right now. These shape the recommendation, but aren't strict filters — the AI weighs them alongside your description.

3
Read the recommendation

You'll get a single book recommendation with three paragraphs of considered context — not a blurb, but a genuine literary case for why this book, for you, now.

4
Ask for another, or share it

Not quite right? Ask for another — it won't repeat itself. Found something great? Copy a shareable link to send to a friend.

Your history

Recommendations are saved locally in your browser — nothing is stored on any server. Your reading history belongs to you and lives only on your device.
